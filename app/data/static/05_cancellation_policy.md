# Cancellation policy

## How late can I cancel my booking for free at CityPark Central?
A booking can be cancelled free of charge when it is cancelled 24 hours or more before the start time. If a booking is cancelled less than 24 hours before the start time but before the booking begins, there is no refund. After the booking has started, the booking cannot be cancelled, and the driver is treated as using the parking period that was booked.

## Can I get a refund at CityPark Central?
Full refunds are only available when the booking is cancelled 24 hours or more before the start time. Cancellations made after this point but before the start time do not receive a refund, and bookings cancelled after the start time cannot be cancelled at all. This rule keeps the booking system consistent and prevents late changes from undermining the facility’s allocation of space.

## What happens if I do not show up for a booking at CityPark Central?
If a driver does not show up and does not cancel before the start time, the booking is treated as a no-show and no refund is due. The no-show rule is applied to keep the booking process fair to other drivers and to protect the facility’s capacity planning. Booking changes and cancellations are only valid when the driver uses the reference and plate together.

## Can I change my booking at CityPark Central?
Booking changes are allowed only before the start time and require the booking reference and the car plate. The booking must still match the vehicle and service conditions, and the reservation cannot be changed after the booking has started. This rule keeps the booking record accurate and ensures that entry and access remain aligned with the active reservation.
