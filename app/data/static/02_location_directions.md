# Location and directions

## Where is CityPark Central located?
CityPark Central is located at 28 Station Square, Crown District, City Centre, B1 7QR. It sits close to the main station approach and the shopping streets in the city centre, making it convenient for commuters and short-stay visitors. The site is placed to support quick arrivals from transport and retail routes while keeping the approach to the facility organised and easy to follow.

## Which entrance is closest to the station?
The Station Entrance is the closest entrance to the station side of the city centre. Drivers approaching from the rail side should follow the station approach signage and stay in the marked entry lane. This route is designed to keep traffic moving smoothly and to reduce confusion for visitors using the station and nearby offices.

## Which entrance is closest to the shopping streets?
The Retail Entrance is the closest entrance to the shopping streets and pedestrian plaza side of the city centre. Drivers arriving from the retail area should follow the retail approach signs and enter through the marked lane for the shopping side. This route gives visitors a simple approach when they are stopping for a short journey or a quick city-centre visit.

## How do drivers reach the upper levels of CityPark Central?
Drivers reach the upper levels of CityPark Central by following the marked internal ramps and signage from the entry lane. The facility is arranged so vehicles move on the ramp system to the correct deck without repeated circulation through the access area. The ramp network is the vehicle route between levels; pedestrian lifts are covered separately in the accessibility section.
