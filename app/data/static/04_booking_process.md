# Booking process

## How do I make a reservation at CityPark Central and what details are needed?
To make a reservation at CityPark Central, the driver selects the booking date, start time, end time, and the required bay type before confirming the booking. Reservations are for a single day, so the start and end time must fall on the same date. The bot confirms the booking immediately, and the driver must provide the booking reference and vehicle plate later for lookups or cancellations.

## What do I need to check in and enter CityPark Central?
At check-in, the driver uses the booking reference and the vehicle registration plate to match the active reservation with number-plate recognition at the entrance. No printed ticket is used; the system reads the plate and checks it against the booking record before it grants access. Drivers should approach the correct entrance and follow the posted lane guidance so the entry process remains secure and efficient.

## What is my booking reference and how is it used at CityPark Central?
A booking reference follows the format CP-XXXXXX and is the key identifier for the reservation. The reference is used to look up the booking, confirm the payment, and support changes or cancellations, but it must always be used together with the car plate. The chatbot itself never shows or cancels a booking without both the booking reference and the vehicle plate.
