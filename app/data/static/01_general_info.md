# General information

## What is CityPark Central?
CityPark Central is a secure multi-level parking facility in the city centre designed for commuters, short-stay visitors, and drivers using nearby offices, retail streets, and civic venues. The site is organised across several levels with clearly marked bays, access lanes, and visitor guidance to make arrival and departure straightforward. CityPark Central is intended to provide a predictable, safe, and orderly parking experience for vehicles, pedestrians, and staff.

## What is the maximum vehicle height at CityPark Central?
The standard maximum vehicle height at CityPark Central is 2.1 metres across the main parking area. The Level 3 low-clearance zone is restricted to vehicles under 1.9 metres and is marked with clear signage for drivers. This helps prevent unnecessary access issues and keeps the internal circulation safe for everyone using the site.

## What parking levels does CityPark Central have?
CityPark Central has four levels: the ground level, Level 1, Level 2, and Level 3. The ground level is used for standard and disabled bays, Level 1 is for standard and compact bays, Level 2 is for standard, compact, and EV bays, and Level 3 includes standard bays and the dedicated low-clearance zone. The layout supports a clear circulation plan and keeps vehicle movement organised across the facility.

## What types of bay are available at CityPark Central?
CityPark Central offers standard bays, compact bays, EV bays, and disabled bays. Standard bays are used for routine vehicle parking, compact bays suit smaller vehicles, EV bays are reserved for active charging, and disabled bays are set aside for accessibility needs. Drivers should always use the bay type that matches their vehicle and the posted instructions for that area.
