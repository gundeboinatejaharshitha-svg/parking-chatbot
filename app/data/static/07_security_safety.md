# Security and safety

## Are there cameras and patrols at CityPark Central?
CityPark Central monitors all levels with CCTV cameras and regular patrols by on-site staff. The cameras support access control and general site monitoring, while the patrols provide a visible response to suspicious activity or operational issues. This combination helps keep the environment secure, predictable, and safe for drivers, pedestrians, and staff.

## What should I do if I see suspicious activity at CityPark Central?
If you see suspicious activity, unusual behaviour, or an unattended item, you should report it immediately to the support desk or the on-site team. The facility encourages quick reporting so staff can respond before a minor issue escalates into a larger safety concern. Prompt action helps protect other users and keeps the site secure for everyone.

## What should I do in an emergency or fire alarm at CityPark Central?
In an emergency or fire alarm, follow the green exit signs and leave the facility by the nearest safe route. Use the stairs rather than the lifts and proceed to the assembly point at the Station Entrance. This procedure is designed to support a quick, orderly evacuation and to keep everyone clear of the vehicle decks and circulation routes.

## Is my vehicle and its contents covered at CityPark Central?
CityPark Central is not liable for loss or damage to vehicles or their contents while they are on site. Drivers are responsible for securing valuables and following the site rules, while the facility focuses on access control, safety monitoring, and operational management. This limitation is part of the site’s risk position and helps keep the car park operating in a controlled and consistent way.
