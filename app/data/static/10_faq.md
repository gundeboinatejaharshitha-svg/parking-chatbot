# FAQ

## What should I do if I lose my booking reference at CityPark Central?
If you lose your booking reference, contact CityPark Central support with the car plate, your name, and the booking date. The support team can then match the booking and help with a lookup or a cancellation request, but the chatbot must never show or cancel a booking without both the booking reference and the car plate. This rule protects bookings and keeps the reservation system secure.

## What should I do if I lose an item at CityPark Central?
If you lose an item in the car park, go to the support desk and report the item, the likely location, and the time of the visit. Lost items are handled through the support desk process, and staff can confirm whether an item has been handed in and how it can be collected. This is the correct route for lost-property queries and prevents guesswork about where an item may have been left.

## Are pets allowed at CityPark Central?
Pets are allowed at CityPark Central only when they are under control, kept on a lead or in an appropriate secure carrier, and do not create a risk to pedestrians, staff, or other drivers. The facility expects owners to keep animals away from restricted or operational spaces and to take responsibility for their behaviour while on site. This keeps the environment safe and comfortable for all users.

## Can motorcycles, vans and large vehicles use CityPark Central?
Motorcycles use compact bays on Level 1 and Level 2 and are not a separate bookable type. Vans, minibuses, trailers, and caravans are refused entry because there is no authorised vehicle area for them. Drivers with larger vehicles should not rely on standard bays or compact bays when the vehicle does not fit the site’s rules and access conditions.

## Can I stay overnight at CityPark Central?
Overnight stays are not allowed unless the booking has been specifically authorised in advance. CityPark Central is designed primarily for day-use and short-stay parking, so drivers should not leave a vehicle overnight without the correct permission. This rule helps keep the facility aligned with the parking plan and prevents misuse of the site.

You can contact CityPark Central support by phone on +44 20 5555 0101 or by email at support@cityparkcentral.example. The support team is available during the customer support desk hours, which is the support-hours label and not the car park opening hours. This is the only place where the phone number and email are listed in the static knowledge base.
