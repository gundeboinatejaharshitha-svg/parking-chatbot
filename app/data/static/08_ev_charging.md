# EV charging

## Where are EV bays at CityPark Central and who can use them?
EV bays are located on Level 2 and are reserved for active charging use. The bays require an active booking for an EV space before the vehicle can use the charging area, and they are not available for general parking. Motorcycles use compact bays on Level 1 and Level 2 rather than separate motorcycle bays, and non-EV vehicles must not use EV bays.

## What are the rules for using an EV bay at CityPark Central?
Drivers using an EV bay must be actively charging the vehicle and must remain within the booked period. Charging sessions are limited to the booked period, and vehicles that finish charging must be moved within the grace period. This helps keep the EV bays available and prevents general parking from blocking legitimate charging users.

## Can non-EV drivers use an EV bay at CityPark Central?
Non-EV drivers must not use EV bays at CityPark Central. These bays are dedicated to electric charging and are only for vehicles that are actively charging under a valid EV booking. Using an EV bay for ordinary parking can block a legitimate charging session and may be treated as a non-compliance issue.
