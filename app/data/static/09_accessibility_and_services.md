# Accessibility and services

## Where are the disabled bays at CityPark Central?
Disabled bays are located on the ground level near the main pedestrian route and the nearest accessible access point. These bays are clearly marked and reserved for drivers with accessibility needs, so they should not be used for general parking. The site keeps them close to the main access points to support easy entry and exit for users who need additional mobility support.

## How do pedestrians get between levels at CityPark Central?
Pedestrians move between levels using the central lift core and the stairs, and the site includes step-free access where required. The lift lobby is the main point for assisted vertical movement, and the route is clearly signed to keep the transition between levels simple. The vehicle ramp system is separate from the pedestrian route and is only used for vehicle circulation.

## How do I request assistance at CityPark Central?
Assistance can be requested through CityPark Central support or via the help points located at each lift lobby. The help points are intended for practical guidance and issues that affect access or support while the driver is on site. This helps users receive quick guidance without needing to guess how to reach the right route or level.

## What other on-site services exist at CityPark Central?
The on-site services are limited to help points and CCTV monitoring, with no shops, no car wash, and no additional retail services on site. These services are intended to support access, visibility, and customer guidance rather than provide extra commercial amenities. The focus remains on safe, clear, and efficient vehicle movement through the facility.
