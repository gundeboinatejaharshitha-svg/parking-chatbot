# Parking rules

## What are the standard parking rules at CityPark Central?
Drivers must park fully within a marked bay and not block access lanes, walkways, ramps, or emergency routes. The speed limit is 5 mph within the facility, and vehicles should keep to the marked circulation pattern without stopping in the wrong area. Drivers have a 15-minute grace period after the booked end time to leave, and staying beyond that grace period is handled as an overstay under the enforcement rule already in this file. These rules help maintain a predictable and safe environment for drivers, pedestrians, and staff.

## Where is stopping or parking prohibited at CityPark Central?
Stopping or parking is prohibited in restricted areas, ramps, internal lanes, emergency exits, and any location marked for operational use. This includes clear access routes, emergency access points, and any section reserved for a special purpose such as disabled or EV use. Drivers should treat all signage and bay markings as the final instruction and avoid using any area that is not clearly intended for general parking.

## What happens if I park outside a marked bay at CityPark Central?
If a driver parks outside a marked bay, the vehicle may receive a warning on the first offence and then a penalty notice if the problem is repeated or serious. This enforcement applies because non-compliant parking creates safety risks and disrupts the site’s circulation plan. Drivers should follow the floor lines, signage, and posted instructions instead of guessing where to leave a vehicle.
