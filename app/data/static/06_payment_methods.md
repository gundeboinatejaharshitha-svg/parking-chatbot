# Payment methods

## Which payment methods are accepted at CityPark Central?
CityPark Central accepts contactless cards, debit cards, major credit cards, and secure online payments made through the booking system. These methods are chosen to match the booking and payment process used before arrival, and they are checked against the active reservation record. Drivers should enter valid card details that match the booking so the payment can be processed without delay.

## Do I pay at the barrier or online at CityPark Central?
Payment is completed online with the booking reference before arrival at CityPark Central, and the barrier checks the reservation against the number plate rather than taking cash or on-site payment. This approach keeps the access process consistent and prevents drivers from being delayed when they reach the facility. The booking is only considered fully confirmed once the payment is accepted.

## What if my payment fails at CityPark Central?
If the payment fails, the booking is not charged and the driver can retry from the booking system or contact support for assistance. The payment record is not confirmed until the transaction is accepted, so the booking system treats it as incomplete rather than as a completed charge. This helps prevent duplicate payments and keeps the driver’s booking record accurate.
